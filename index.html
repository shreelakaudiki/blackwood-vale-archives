ABOUT THE ARCHIVE

The Blackwood & Vale historical archive preserves selected catalogues, lot descriptions and administrative records from the firm's early years in London.

Some records remain incomplete. Catalogue numbers and descriptions follow the terminology used in the surviving documents.
